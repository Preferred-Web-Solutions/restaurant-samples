About us

A small crew that answers its phone.

Why we started.

Dale Harlan roofed for other companies for twelve years before starting Tallow Ridge. He wanted to give homeowners what he never saw enough of: a straight answer, a written price, and a crew that shows up when it said it would.

“If the roof does not need replacing, we will tell you that and fix what is wrong.”Dale Harlan, owner

The owner, story and quote are invented for this sample. On a real site, they come from the owner.

How we work

  • Free inspection with photos.
  • A written quote before any work starts.
  • The same crew from start to finish.
  • Cleanup every day, with a magnet sweep for nails.
  • A final walk-through before the last invoice.
