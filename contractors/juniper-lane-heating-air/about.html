About us

A small shop that explains what it finds.

Why we started.

Maria Okafor spent ten years as a service tech and kept hearing the same thing: “Nobody told me what was wrong.” She started Juniper Lane to show customers the problem, quote the price first, and let them decide.

“If a repair is not worth it, we will tell you, and we will tell you what is.”Maria Okafor, owner

The owner, story and quote are invented for this sample. On a real site, they come from the owner.

How we work

  • Diagnosis first, with photos.
  • A written quote before any repair.
  • Options, not pressure.
  • Shoe covers and a tidy van.
  • A test of the system before we leave.
