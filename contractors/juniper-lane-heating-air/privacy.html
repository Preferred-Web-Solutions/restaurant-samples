Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. Juniper Lane Heating & Air is a fictional HVAC company. This site is a design sample made by Preferred Web Solutions (PWS), and PWS is responsible for it.

What this site collects

Your cookie choice. When you choose an option in the cookie banner, your choice, the date and the policy version are saved in your own browser (local storage). It is not sent to us. See the Cookie Policy.

Demo forms. The forms on this sample are demos. They are not connected, and nothing you type in them is sent anywhere.

Website issue reports. If you use the Report a website issue form, what you enter (your message, the page, and your name and email if you choose to give them) is sent to PWS through Formspree, a form-processing service. Formspree processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. We use it only to fix the problem and reply to you.

Hosting. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify you.

What this site does not do

This site does not use analytics, advertising pixels, social media trackers or third-party fonts. It does not sell or share personal information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To make a request, email preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. You can change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie settings button at the bottom of every page.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version number and date above. If the change affects cookies, the site will ask for your choice again.
