Cookie Policy

This page lists everything this website stores in your browser, and every outside service it contacts. It was checked against the site's actual behavior on November 5, 2026.

This site sets no cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking tools, in any category. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so no outside font or image services are contacted when you load a page.

Outside services

The only outside service is Formspree, which receives a report only when you submit the Report a website issue form. Nothing is sent to Formspree when you simply visit a page.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Use the Cookie settings button at the bottom of any page to see or change your choice. If we ever add analytics or marketing tools, they will stay off unless you accept them, and this page will list them first.

Clearing stored data in your browser

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Delete browsing data, then choose Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari (Mac): Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, then remove this site.
  • Safari (iPhone): Settings, Apps, Safari, Advanced, Website Data, then remove this site.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy & Security, Cookies and Site Data, Manage Data.
  • Edge: Settings, Privacy, search and services, Clear browsing data.
