# Sample-site inspiration brief (Nov 5 2026)
Method: WebFetch for structure and copy (built-in browser not available in this session), so type and color were NOT seen live. Each takeaway is from page text only. Sites that failed or refused (Capital Grille: robots) were replaced.

## Diner (Hollis Street Diner)
Large: First Watch (full-bleed food hero, one wait-list/order action, witty copy; avoid: location + waitlist modals, repeated email signup). Snooze (gift-card-first home; playful copy; avoid: promo banner as first thing).
Mid: Bubby's (utility bar with address + phone, hero gallery with pause control; avoid: duplicate header/footer nav, conflicting booking instructions, 7am-11pm time dropdown). Lou Mitchell's (loud diner-sign type, history story, hours block; avoid: dense price lists on home, award-badge clutter).
Small/independent: Camp Washington Chili, Cincinnati (heritage "since 1940", corner location, plain menu link; avoid: confusing hours line, empty headings). Webflow showcase "Cafe Rosa"/"211 cafe" (each section tells a story; photo-led).
Take: food-first hero, address+hours strip right under it, one order action, a story section with real specifics, hours that never contradict. Avoid: popups, badge walls, repeated CTAs with different wording.
Direction: guest-check/order-rail motif (kept from v1), but multi-page: Home, Menu, Pies, About, Contact. Home = split hero (type + photo) with a printed guest check, hours strip, the rail of daily specials, weekend headline dish, morning slideshow, pie teaser.

## Upscale (Ashgrove)
Large/regional: Jeff Ruby's (founder quote, celebration framing; avoid: promo modules stacked, reservation link to "#"). Capital Grille (blocked).
Mid: Alinea (big atmospheric photo, three named experiences, one persistent BOOK A TABLE; avoid: repeated nav, missing alt text, awards dominating). Cosme (bold uppercase dish names, lowercase ingredients, whole-dollar prices, short About; avoid: seasonal popup, typos, stale reservation date). Eleven Madison Park (editorial photos, farm story, one Resy button; avoid: duplicate gallery, stale hardcoded date).
Small/independent: Sotto, Cincinnati (basement wine cellar as the identity, hero gallery with pause, plain reservation form; avoid: time slots outside service hours, duplicate nav). Osteria 60 (Webflow showcase: gray + gold, plating-led photography).
Take: restraint, one reservation action, the room as identity, prices in whole dollars, menu typeset as typography. Avoid: awards walls, popups, reservation slots outside service hours.
Direction: dark moss + brass, Bodoni, tasting card. Pages: Home, Menu & wine, Private dining, About, Contact (reserve).

## Mexican (Cantina Zafiro)
Large: Rosa Mexicano (hero video, tableside-guacamole hook, sun/star illustration system; avoid: dead "#" links, repeated carousel, stale copyright). Tacombi (looping hero with pause, "Come for tacos. Stay for the vibe.", Spanish woven in; avoid: competing banners, vague "Order Order", repeated CTAs).
Mid: Big Star Chicago (patio/food/sign photography, order dropdown per location; avoid: no prices). Cosme (see above).
Small/independent: Casa Mx, Mason OH (owner benchmark: candid food/room photos, slideshow with pause, hours block; avoid: five stacked popups, repeated CTAs, typos, blank meta description). Isses (Webflow showcase: muted greens and pinks instead of loud red/yellow).
Take: bold playful headlines with some Spanish, food close-ups, ordering via a dropdown, catering/party blocks. Avoid: popups, stacked promos, clip-art clutter.
Direction: lotería-card motif (kept), bright magenta/marigold/teal on cream. Pages: Home, Menu & drinks, Catering & parties, About, Contact.

## Roofing contractor sample
Large/regional: MHI Roof (rating in hero, warranty/accreditation, lead form above the fold; avoid: countdown at zero, off-brand link, inconsistent reviews, many badges). Mr. Handyman (van photo hero, one Get Started + phone; avoid: placeholder 0/5 rating, inconsistent years).
Mid: Janney Roofing (three-step process, gallery filter by roof type, license numbers in footer, founder quote; avoid: stale storm banner, repeated superlatives). Bumble Roofing / Munz Roofing (service-area + years + financing in hero).
Small independent: Chet's Roofing (before/after pairs, family-owned, one "Get a Free Estimate"; avoid: two phone numbers, wrong city on page). Fahey Roofing (family-owned, storm tarping offer first, four-step process; avoid: typos, three phone numbers, "best" claims).
Take: one free-inspection action, a plain 3-4 step process, job gallery by roof type, license/insurance block (marked invented for a fictional company). Avoid: more than one phone number, superlatives with no proof, stale storm banners.
Direction: roofer's job-site clipboard / inspection checklist motif; charcoal + safety orange... see build for final.

## HVAC contractor sample
Large: Service Experts (zip-code finder, 24/7 scheduling, license block; avoid: crowded offer carousel, inconsistent destinations). Roto-Rooter (phone-first, 8 service cards; avoid: duplicate blocks, mega-menu, competing CTAs, "loading reviews").
Mid: Bradham Brothers (family-owned since 1975, many badges; avoid: duplicate copy, template placeholders, banner clutter). Trademark Mechanical (24/7 bar, branded truck, license numbers in footer; avoid: unrendered template placeholders, stale offer dates).
Small independent: Medley Heating & Air (team photo, review counts, transparent pricing, employee-not-subcontractor; avoid: three phone numbers, dense SMS legal text). NRG / Skylake (clear service menu and service area).
Take: one schedule action with phone beside it, honest pricing section, real team story, license in footer. Avoid: fake countdowns, repeated banners, review ratings that are not real.
Direction: thermostat-dial / seasonal-comfort motif, navy + warm amber, Fraunces-free (self-hosted open fonts only).
