Nuestra historia

Recipes from our family table.

The Zafiro family brought their recipes from Puebla in 1998 and sold tamales from a cooler on Saturday mornings. The cantina opened in 2011, and the tortilla press has not stopped since.

“If the tortilla is not fresh, nothing else matters.”Lupe Zafiro, owner
